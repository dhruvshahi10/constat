Status. Pramana holds no ISO/IEC 42001 certification and no AI management system certification of any kind. This document is a planning artifact describing intended alignment. It is not evidence of certification and is deliberately typed as a roadmap so that the engine's own certification gate refuses to cite it in support of a certification claim.

Why this document exists in this form. It is also the clearest demonstration of the product's central control. Asked whether Pramana is ISO 42001 certified, the engine retrieves this document, recognises the question as a certification claim, observes that the only supporting source is typed roadmap rather than certificate or attestation, discards the citation and refuses. The refusal is generated, not written.

Clauses currently aligned in practice. AI system scope and intended use are documented. Roles and accountability rest with a single named owner. Data sources for AI operation are inventoried and governed, with approval status, ownership and validity dates enforced in code. AI system impact considerations are addressed by fail-closed refusal design and by human accountability for released statements. Logging of AI operation is implemented as a hash-chained, append-only record of every state transition and every human decision.

Clauses not yet met. There is no independent audit, no management review cycle, no formal risk treatment plan under an AI management system, no documented competence and training programme, and no certification body engagement. A Stage 1 audit is not scheduled.

Honest characterisation. The correct description of Pramana today is a pre-pilot system whose AI governance controls are implemented in code and demonstrable by test, and whose management-system documentation is not certified by anyone.
