# Human-in-the-Loop Design and Approval Plan

A person signs, not the engine. Constat establishes whether a statement is supported by an approved, in-force document. It does not take responsibility for making the statement. A questionnaire answer is a material security representation, and a named person accepts it before it reaches a buyer.

Approval cannot manufacture evidence. Approval is only offered for an answer that already survived the gates. Where nothing was released, a reviewer may reject the question or author an answer themselves; an answer a human writes is recorded as human-authored and is labelled as such in the answer contract and in the delivered workbook. A reader can always distinguish what the system supported from what a person asserted.

Review is part of the record. Every decision writes an event onto the same hash-chained audit log as the run it decided on, naming the reviewer, the action, the resulting state and the citations in force at that moment. Altering a historical entry invalidates the chain from that point onward, and this is verified by test.

Approval of evidence is itself a human act. An ingested document is not citable because ingestion succeeded. It becomes citable when a named person approves it, and that approval is recorded.
