# ISO/IEC 42001 Alignment Roadmap

Status. Constat holds no ISO/IEC 42001 certification and no AI management system certification of any kind. This document is a planning artifact describing intended alignment. It is deliberately typed as a roadmap so that the certification gate refuses to cite it in support of any certification claim.

Why this document exists in this form. It is also the clearest demonstration of the product's central control. Asked whether Constat holds ISO/IEC 42001 certification, the engine retrieves this document, recognises a certification claim, observes that the only supporting source is a roadmap rather than a certificate or attestation, discards the citation and refuses. The refusal is generated, not written by hand.

Clauses aligned in practice today. AI system scope and intended use are documented. Accountability rests with a single named owner. Data sources for AI operation are inventoried and governed, with approval status, ownership and validity dates enforced in code. Impact considerations are addressed by fail-closed refusal design and by human accountability for released statements. Logging of AI operation is implemented as a hash-chained, append-only record of every state transition and every human decision.

Clauses not met. There is no independent audit, no management review cycle, no formal risk treatment plan under an AI management system, no documented competence and training programme, and no engagement with a certification body. No Stage 1 audit is scheduled.

Honest characterisation. Constat today is a pre-pilot system whose AI governance controls are implemented in code and demonstrable by test, and whose management-system documentation is certified by nobody.
