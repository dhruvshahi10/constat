# Access Control Policy

Access to production systems follows least privilege and role-based access control. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all workforce access to corporate and production environments, including VPN and SSO.

Access rights are reviewed quarterly by system owners; review evidence is retained. Access is revoked within 24 hours of termination as part of the offboarding runbook.
