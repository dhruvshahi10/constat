# Information Security Policy

Acme maintains a formal information security program governed by this policy, reviewed and approved annually by the CISO and executive leadership. The program is aligned to ISO/IEC 27001 control themes and covers all production systems and personnel.

## Risk management
Formal risk assessments are performed at least annually and upon significant change. Results are tracked in the risk register with named owners and remediation dates.

## Logging and monitoring
Production systems emit centralized audit logs retained for security analysis. Logs are protected against tampering and monitored for anomalous activity with 24x7 alerting.

## Endpoint protection
Company-managed endpoints run centrally managed anti-malware and disk encryption, with automatic patching enforced by MDM.
