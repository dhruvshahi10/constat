# Subprocessor List

Acme production infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services (primary: us-east-1, secondary: eu-west-1). Additional subprocessors: Datadog (observability), Stripe (payments), Postmark (transactional email).

Customers are notified of subprocessor changes at least 30 days in advance via the trust portal subscription.
