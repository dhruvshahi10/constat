# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan

The platform targets a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours and a recovery point objective (RPO) of 1 hour for production services, using cross-region replication and automated failover runbooks.

DR procedures are tested at least annually; the most recent full failover test was completed in March 2026 and met both objectives.
