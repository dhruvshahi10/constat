# ISO 27001 Certification Roadmap

Acme is pursuing ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification. Stage 1 audit is targeted for Q1 2027 and certification decision for Q2 2027. Gap assessment is complete; 34 of 41 planned controls are implemented.

Acme is not currently ISO 27001 certified. This document is a plan, not a certificate.
