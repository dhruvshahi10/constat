# Incident Response Plan

Acme operates a documented incident response process with defined severity levels, an on-call rotation and escalation paths. Confirmed incidents affecting customer data trigger customer notification without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirmation, consistent with contractual and regulatory obligations.

Post-incident reviews are mandatory for SEV-1 and SEV-2 events, with corrective actions tracked to closure. The plan is tested at least annually via tabletop exercise; the most recent exercise was completed in March 2026.
