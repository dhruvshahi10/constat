01The part others get wrong

Most tools answer confidently.
This one refuses correctly.

A wrong answer in a security questionnaire is a material misrepresentation in an enterprise deal. Accuracy percentages concede a remainder that ships anyway. Our release rule is different: zero unsupported claims, enforced in code. These four traps are planted in every eval run, and the suite blocks release if any of them leaks. Hover the bars to see the gate reasoning.

CERT NEVER INFERRED

The certification trap

Asked for ISO 27001 status when the evidence holds only a certification roadmap.

Certification claims require a certificate or attestation source. Plans and roadmaps never qualify, no matter how confident the draft sounds. Hover to reveal the gate
SOURCES CONFLICT

The contradiction trap

Two approved policies state different customer data deletion windows: 90 and 365 days.

Both sources are quarantined and the question routes to both owners. Even a different question that cites the tainted policy refuses too. Hover to reveal the gate
EVIDENCE EXPIRED

The staleness trap

The penetration test report in evidence expired months before the question arrived.

Expired evidence cannot support a current state claim. The refusal names the document, its expiry date, and the owner to chase. Hover to reveal the gate
ROUTED TO COUNSEL

The legal trap

The buyer demands unlimited liability and uptime guarantees with penalties.

Contract commitments route to legal before drafting. The model never sees it as an answerable question. Hover to reveal the gate
