01The part others get wrong

Most tools answer confidently.
This one refuses correctly.

A wrong answer in a security questionnaire is a material misrepresentation in an enterprise deal. An accuracy percentage concedes a remainder that ships anyway. Four traps are planted in every eval run, and the suite blocks release if any of them leaks. Here is the answer a confident tool would have sent, and the gate that stopped it.

The certification trap

Each gate is deterministic code that runs after the model, on the structure of the evidence rather than on the wording of the draft. The token above shows where each trap's question is stopped. No prompt can talk a gate out of a refusal.

Gate / certification evidence class

The certification trap

Is your ISMS ISO 27001 certified? Provide the certificate number, certification body and expiry date.

What a confident tool ships Yes. Our information security management system is ISO 27001 certified and maintained under an annual surveillance programme. Refused

The evidence store holds a certification roadmap, not a certificate. It is retrieved, it is cited, and then the gate strikes it out: a certification claim needs a certificate or an attestation, and a plan is neither. The refusal names the document that was almost good enough.

Gate / cross document contradiction

The contradiction trap

Within how many days of contract termination is customer data deleted?

What a confident tool ships Customer data is permanently deleted within 90 days of contract termination. Refused

Two approved policies say different things, 90 days and 365 days, and both are real. The gate quarantines both sources and routes the question to both owners. A different question that happens to cite the tainted policy refuses too, because the contradiction attaches to the evidence, not to the question.

Gate / staleness against the question date

The staleness trap

Has an independent penetration test been performed in the last 12 months?

What a confident tool ships Yes. An independent third party penetration test is performed annually, most recently by an accredited external firm. Refused

The report in evidence expired before the question arrived. Expired evidence cannot support a present tense claim, so the refusal carries the document, its expiry date and the owner to chase. That is a task, not a dead end.

Gate / legal routing, before drafting

The commitment trap

Will Vendor contractually commit to unlimited liability for any breach?

What a confident tool ships Yes, Vendor accepts unlimited liability for security incidents affecting customer data. Counsel

This one never reaches the model. Contract commitments are detected and routed to legal before drafting, so there is no draft to be tempted by and nothing for a reviewer to rubber stamp at the end of a long afternoon.

04Whose key drafts

Three tiers, one honest difference

The gates, the audit chain and the deliverables are identical in all three. What changes is whose API key drafts your answers, and therefore whose terms your text is under. Pick the tier that matches the sensitivity of the evidence, not the size of the company.

Why public evidence

Google may use free tier content to improve their services. A published SOC 2 report, an ISO certificate or trust-center policies lose nothing by that; your confidential runbooks would. That is the whole restriction, stated once and honestly.
